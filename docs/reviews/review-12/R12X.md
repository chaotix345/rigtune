- **JournalCache:**
  - The key reads `writes` before the attributes, and Journal increments it after `AtomicFiles.writeString`, so a concurrent write always makes before != after (never kept) or a later key mismatch.

  - Snapshot lists are immutable (`toList()`).
  - MISSING and UNREADABLE are never kept.
  - `computeIfAbsent` on the synchronized map is atomic.
  - Retention: one parsed history per session, bounded by the journal cap. Acceptable.
- **SettingsWatch.failed** is touched only on the render thread (the listener).
- **Done marks:** only the helper writes them, under ApplyLock, through the forcing writer.
